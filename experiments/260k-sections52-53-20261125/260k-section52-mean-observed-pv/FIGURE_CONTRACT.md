# Section 5.2 — MLP means and last observed PV

- User request: the decision controller must not access the empirical noise bank. It may use only the three MLP means and the latest valid PV observation at or before the decision time. Estimated PV energy is observed power times mean duration, not an integral.
- Decision: E_PV_hat = alpha * P_raw(last_timestamp <= t) * m_T; E_s_hat = max(m_dem - E_PV_hat,0). The LYA ratio, decreasing phase weights (100,10,1), and sigma_e=0.24 remain the same. This plug-in shortfall estimate is not claimed to be the exact conditional expectation of actual shortfall.
- Environment: jointly sampled empirical noise, mean-preserving/physical-boundary calibration, actual duration and demand, and PVDatabase integration over the realized epoch. Actual observations update J, phase, Q and the clock.
- Causality: no interpolated midnight value, future sample, future PV integral, residual scenario or donor identifier enters the controller. Hold the latest measurement across an observation gap, explicitly record its age. Use the public observations() API, including the latest valid preceding-date observation for midnight initialization.
- Scope: all original 240 dates, midnight, 30 tasks x 4 epochs, 32 paired noise realizations, same six baselines, same global PV70 factor and all five capacity sensitivities. Select only V on the original 48 tuning dates and seven-value grid; retain the 192-date confirmation and quality subsets.
- Layout: existing OR Figure 8 composite, 240 chronological PV-energy cells, seven mean TARS bars with paired week-bootstrap intervals, all 1,680 daily DWAE–TARS points. Use LYA, TARS=time-averaged renewable shortfall, DWAE=duration-weighted average error. Preserve all observations and disclose any constraint violations.
- Backend/export: Python/matplotlib, Times New Roman, 9–10 pt, grayscale, 6.5-inch width; PDF/SVG and PNG, 600 dpi TIFF for main figure. Old experiments and frozen model stay unchanged in their original directories.
- Integrity checks: independent Python decision/trajectory replay; alter future PV and environment noise while holding decision inputs fixed; verify identical selected actions and scores. Verify actual environment responses still change with noise. Check means, day coverage, measurement ages and reused baseline hashes. Historical perfect-information LYA is a labeled control, not a new baseline.
