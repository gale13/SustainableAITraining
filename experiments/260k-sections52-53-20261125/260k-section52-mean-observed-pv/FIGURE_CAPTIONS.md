# Figure 8. PV Generation and Algorithm Performance with Observed-Power Decisions.

*Notes.* TARS denotes time-averaged renewable shortfall and DWAE denotes duration-weighted average error. The 260K simulator executes 30 tasks of four epochs per policy from midnight, with 32 paired empirical-noise realizations on each of 240 dates. LYA uses decreasing phase weights (100,10,1), V=0.1, and a DWAE target of 0.24. Decisions use only the three MLP means and the latest valid PV measurement available by the decision time; estimated PV energy is this scaled power times mean epoch duration. Environmental outcomes use mean plus empirical joint noise and integrate actual PV over realized duration. (a) Gray intensity denotes full-day PV generation. Crosses indicate a smaller daily mean TARS for the best feasible baseline, circles indicate numerical ties, and unmarked cells indicate a smaller TARS for LYA. (b) Bars are equal-date mean TARS, with 95% intervals from 2,000 paired calendar-week bootstrap samples. Hatching identifies observed DWAE violations. (c) All 240 daily means per policy and seven overall means are shown; the dashed line denotes DWAE=0.24. The common PV multiplier α=4.19288 is calibrated on 48 tuning dates; 192 dates are excluded from tuning. Official unlimited-gap interpolation is used only by the environment, while the controller holds the latest actual measurement. Forty-five dates involve support gaps exceeding five minutes; observation-age and quality-subset results are reported separately.

Supplementary figures use the same configurations at the five fixed capacity multipliers and the PV70 factor. Only V is tuned on the original tuning split. All daily observations are retained; numerical daily wins do not imply per-day significance.
