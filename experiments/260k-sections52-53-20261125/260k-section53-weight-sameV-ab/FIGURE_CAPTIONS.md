**Effect of decreasing phase weights at the same Lyapunov parameter V.** (a) Percentage TARS reduction relative to Fixed=(100,100,100). (b) Percentage reduction relative to Increasing=(100,1000,10000). In each panel the denominator is the comparator's mean TARS at that same V; positive values favor the decreasing schedule. Both panels use sigma_e=.235, all 240 PV dates, 32 paired noise realizations per date, and 30 four-epoch tasks per run. Points are equal-date means after averaging realizations within each date; shading indicates pointwise 95% paired calendar-week bootstrap intervals from 2,000 resamples. The four decreasing schedules and their runs are shared across panels. Decisions use only MLP means and the latest historical PV power times mean duration; the environment uses empirical joint noise and actual-window PV integration. All 17 V settings, including zero and negative effects, are retained. Lines connect evaluated settings without smoothing. The improvements depend on V and do not establish uniform superiority or superiority after separately tuning V.
